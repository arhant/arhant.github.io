    About

    I'm a Senior Software Engineer and Tech Lead at Cox Automotive, where I've spent 8+ years in the Finance Services division. We build the credit application, lender connectivity, contracting, and document signing platforms used by about 10,000 US dealerships and major lenders.

    I started by architecting the flagship Credit Application and Leads APIs and pioneering AWS Lambda, Serverless Framework, and Terraform adoption at Cox. Today I lead a team of 6 engineers and set direction across event-driven architecture, security and identity, partner integrations, and the deployment and reliability standards the team runs on.
