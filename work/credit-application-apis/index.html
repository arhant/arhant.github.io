Credit Application & Leads APIs

The flagship APIs that connected 800 dealerships to a centralized lender network, built while pioneering AWS Lambda, Serverless Framework, and Terraform at Cox.

In short

I architected and shipped the flagship Credit Application and Leads APIs, connecting 800 dealerships to a centralized lender network and generating $16M+ in first-year revenue. Along the way I introduced a CI/CD pipeline that moved production releases from monthly to every sprint.

Context

This was founding platform work in Cox Automotive's Finance Services division, which builds the credit application, lender connectivity, contracting, and document signing platforms used by about 10,000 US dealerships and major lenders.

Dealerships needed a single way to reach a centralized lender network with credit applications and leads.

What I did

  • Architected and shipped the flagship Credit Application and Leads APIs.
  • Pioneered adoption of AWS Lambda, Serverless Framework, and Terraform at Cox.
  • Introduced a CI/CD pipeline that moved production releases from monthly to every sprint (about 150% more throughput).

Running it in production

In the same period I set the operational standard for Finance Services APIs at a 99.995% Platinum-tier SLA, with automated failover, PagerDuty alerting, and resiliency game days.

On the Credit Application Lambda, I also resolved a high-severity production incident during a partner traffic surge by tracing TCP connection resets, Lambda timeouts, and exhausted retries, then shipped persistent HTTP sessions with exponential backoff and wrote the runbook now reused across services.

Result

800 dealerships connected to a centralized lender network, $16M+ in first-year revenue, and production releases every sprint instead of monthly.
