Spec-driven development with AI agents

How I led my team's adoption of AI-assisted development: spec-driven development with GitHub Spec Kit, with human review and AI cost tracking built in.

Where
Cox Automotive · Finance Services
My part
Led the adoption; wrote the first specifications
Team
6 engineers

In short

I introduced spec-driven development with GitHub Spec Kit to my team and wrote its first specifications. It is now the standard way new features are planned.

Microservices built and reviewed with AI tools
10+
Engineers on the team I lead
6

Context

I'm a Senior Software Engineer and Tech Lead at Cox Automotive, in the Finance Services division. We build the credit application, lender connectivity, contracting, and document signing platforms used by about 10,000 US dealerships and major lenders.

The team needed a consistent, reviewable way to plan and build features with AI tools, with quality and cost kept under control.

What I did

  • Introduced spec-driven development with GitHub Spec Kit and wrote the team's first specifications.
  • Use Claude Code, MCP integrations, and AI agents daily to design, build, test, and review production code across 10+ microservices.
  • Promote responsible, cost-aware AI use, with three guardrails:

  • Human review

    AI-generated specs and code are reviewed by a person before release.

  • Tracked spend

    AI spend is tracked on enterprise dashboards.

  • The right model

    The model is matched to the task.

My role and the team's

I lead and mentor a team of 6 engineers, and run sprint and quarterly planning, design reviews, and production support. I led the adoption and wrote the first specifications; the team now plans new features from specs.

Result

Spec-driven development is now the standard way new features are planned on the team.
